    FAQ

    Quick answers before you trust the numbers.

    Do my numbers get sent anywhere?

    No. Everything happens inside this page in your browser. Your answers are only saved on your own device, and pressing "Start over" at the bottom wipes them away.

    How do you know what each app charges?

    They're typical prices, and apps change them now and then. Press "Show the fee numbers we used" in step 2 to see exactly what we counted, and change anything that doesn't match what your app charges you.

    Fresha doesn't charge for bookings from my own page — do you know that?

    Bookings marked as direct, own link, website, walk-in, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram or referral are counted as "Booked direct" — no app takes a cut of those. Bookings marked Fresha or Treatwell are counted under that app. You'll see the split in the table.

    What's a no-show?

    A no-show is someone who booked but never came. If your list marks them (like "No show"), we leave them out of your earnings and show you how much they cost you instead.

    My app isn't in the list — can I still use this?

    Yes. Tick Another app in step 1, type your bookings for it, and tell us what it charges in step 2.

    Why look at the year and not just the month?

    Fees feel small because they come out bit by bit. Add up a whole year and it's often thousands. Run your numbers above and see what a year of fees could buy instead — a holiday, a new chair, or a big advertising budget.
